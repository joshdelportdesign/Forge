Made for steady progress. Exam: 20 November 2026 · Africa/Johannesburg

Give Bevel a home.

On your iPhone, open this page in Safari, then:

  1. Tap Sharein Safari’s menu or toolbar.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. You may need to scroll through the actions or tap More.
  3. Keep Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.

FORGE will appear with Bevel’s icon and open in an app-style window. Internet access is needed to load the app; this update does not add offline caching.
YOUR STUDY TIME

Add your study sessions.

Export the schedule you reviewed on the Calendar page. Existing calendar conflicts have not been checked.

  1. Save any changes to your availability, then download the study calendar below.
  2. On your Mac, open Calendar → File → Import and choose the downloaded .ics file.
  3. Import into a dedicated FORGE calendar in your iCloud account to see sessions on your iPhone too.

On iPhone? If the downloaded file does not offer an import option, open FORGE on your Mac and follow these steps. Repeated imports may create duplicates.

Connect directly on a Mac

Run python3 server.py from your local FORGE folder, open the private link printed in Terminal, then connect Apple Calendar there. The local helper checks busy times and adds or updates sessions after review.
